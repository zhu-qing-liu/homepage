Short Biography

Zhuqing (Zuki) Liu is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas (UNT). She received her Ph.D. degree from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University in 2024.

Immediate Openings for Ph.D. Students: Openings for Ph.D. students are available and expected to be filled as early as Spring 2027 or in Fall 2027. Please see the Research page to learn about my research areas and interests. If you are interested, please send me your CV/Resume, transcripts, publications (if available) and GRE/TOEFL scores (if available) for consideration. I apologize for not being able to respond to every inquiry, but that does not mean I am not considering your application. Please also refer to the UNT CSE graduate admissions site for detailed application instructions.

Research Interests

  • AI Security and Large Language Models: poisoning and backdoor attacks, responsibility attribution, secure retrieval-augmented generation, LLM agent forensics
  • First-Order Stochastic Optimization for Machine Learning: bilevel, min-max, and multi-objective learning with provable convergence and sample complexity
  • Communication Efficiency in Distributed/Federated/Decentralized Learning: Byzantine robustness, differential privacy, split and asynchronous federated learning
